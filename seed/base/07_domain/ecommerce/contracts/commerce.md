# Commerce

- **The chain is `catalog → cart → order → payment → fulfillment`, each link its own machine:** carts are cheap and disposable; orders are contracts; payments follow the money law; fulfillment follows the item's capability (stock+shipping, instant-digital, service-delivery). State advances through guarded transitions emitting events — never a `status` UPDATE from a controller.
- **An order is a snapshot, not a join:** captured prices, names, taxes, discounts, and addresses freeze into the order rows at placement — catalog edits tomorrow must not rewrite yesterday's receipts. The catalog is live; the order is history.
- **Stock is a concurrency problem first:** reservation at checkout-open with expiry (released by sweeper), atomic decrement under lock/version at placement, oversell impossible by construction — not by apology email. Digital/unlimited items declare the capability and skip the machinery.
- **Pricing is a pipeline with recorded math:** base → type/seasonal rules → quantity tiers → coupons → taxes — each step a named stage writing its contribution into the order's totals; a support agent reads WHY the number is 240, not just that it is. Coupons are constrained data (scope, window, usage caps, stacking rules), validated server-side at placement — never trusted from the cart payload.
- **Checkout is idempotent end to end:** the placement endpoint under an idempotency key, payment through the local-first saga, confirmation on the webhook — a double-click, a retry, or a duplicate webhook must never produce two orders or two charges.
- **Refunds, cancellations, and returns are transitions with policy:** each declares its legal source states, its money consequence (ledger entries through the payment port), and its stock consequence (restock or not) — policy resolved from data (the item's/vendor's cancellation policy), never hardcoded per case.
- **Multi-vendor carts split honestly:** one checkout, per-vendor order groups, per-vendor fulfillment states, platform commission ledgered at capture — the buyer sees one purchase; the books see the truth.
- Taxes and totals compute server-side only, from the address + tax rules in data; every client-sent total is a claim the server recomputes and refuses on mismatch.
